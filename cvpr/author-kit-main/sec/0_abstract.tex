\begin{abstract}
Self-supervised learning (SSL) has emerged as a powerful paradigm for feature extraction in computer vision, particularly in domains where labeled data is scarce or expensive to acquire. In this work, we present a comprehensive study on the application of the DINO (self-distillation with no labels) framework to high-resolution aerial imagery for agricultural monitoring, specifically targeting poultry farm detection. We implement a Domain-Adaptive Pre-Training (DAPT) strategy using a Vision Transformer (ViT-Base) backbone optimized for remote sensing data through a high-throughput WebDataset pipeline. Our method incorporates a composite loss function encompassing DINO cross-entropy, iBOT masked patch prediction, and KoLeo regularization to maintain representation entropy. Extensive downstream evaluations demonstrate that our self-supervised pre-trained model achieves highly competitive segmentation performance (mAP50 of 0.977) while exhibiting substantial label-efficiency and robustness in few-shot scenarios, proving its efficacy as a foundation model for remote sensing applications.
\end{abstract}
